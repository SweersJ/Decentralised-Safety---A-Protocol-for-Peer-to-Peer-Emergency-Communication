## Formal verification textual message

All payload and pending-message ownership invariants hold together.

## Explanation

This aggregate invariant is the conjunction of three independently documented safety predicates. It requires payload messages to remain traceable to sender ownership and either pending or receiver knowledge, while pending messages also remain owned by the sender. Its counterexample should be diagnosed through the failed constituent predicate.
